Premium Matrix

A hypothetical premium-rate grid: strikes down the rows, days to expiration across the columns, every cell showing the Black-Scholes price and the premium rate — the move the underlying must make to reach breakeven. No market data is loaded; every number below is computed in your browser from the four inputs.

Bid-ask width as a percent of mid. 0 = trade at mid.
Both sides share the same premium-rate formulas.

Premium rate — call: (strike + premium - price) / price; put: (price - strike + premium) / price. Mid is the Black-Scholes theoretical value; the fill moves half the spread away from it, so a seller's rate is always lower than a buyer's.
