Premium Matrix

A hypothetical premium-rate grid: strikes down the rows, days to expiration across the columns, every cell showing the Black-Scholes price and the premium rate — the move the underlying must make to reach breakeven. No market data is loaded; every number below is computed in your browser from the four inputs.

Hypothetical Inputs
Underlying spot used for every strike.
Annualised, 0.1 - 500.
Annualised, -5 - 50.
Bid-ask width as a percent of mid, 0 - 100. The fill moves half of it off the mid, floored at $0.01 a side.

Premium rate — call: (strike + premium - price) / price; put: (price - strike + premium) / price. Mid is the Black-Scholes theoretical value; the fill moves half the spread away from it — at least $0.01, the smallest tick an option trades on, so cheap wing premiums still show a gap — and a seller's rate is therefore always lower than a buyer's. Flip the Side switch above the grid to price the other side of the book.

横轴每一列是一个到期期限，列头三行分别是：31D 为距到期天数， ±7.1% 为该期限内标的的 1σ 预期波动（约 68% 概率落在 ±7.1% 区间内， 公式为 IV × √(DTE / 365)），再往下 CALL / PUT 把该列左右两半标出来 —— 每个格子里，左半是 Call（价格在上、溢价率在下），右半是 Put。 纵轴每行是一个行权价：第一列是行权价，第二列是该行权价相对参考期限的 σ 倍数。 悬停会高亮整列与整行，点击某一列可把它设为 σ 参考列。

请至少打开一个数值开关（Price / Prem. rate）和一个方向开关（Call / Put）。

设置上方四个输入后点击 Recalculate，即可生成溢价率矩阵。
